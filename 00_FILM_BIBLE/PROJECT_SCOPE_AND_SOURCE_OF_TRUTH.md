## Minimal update workflow

- Shot generated/reviewed/approved: update the Shot Register and project status.
- New reusable character/location/prop: also update the Asset Register.
- Story/prompt/continuity changed: update only the affected scene breakdown.
- Major owner decision or milestone: append one row to Change History.
- Do not duplicate the same status in multiple boards, checklists and chronological diaries.

## Production safeguards

One output per shot by default. Every new render needs separate explicit owner authorization. Immediately before generation, recheck the live displayed price, settings, exact references and verbatim prompt. Stop if anything differs.

Google Flow is not live-synced to GitHub. Owner-supplied Flow reports and uploaded media are recorded manually; distinguish reported details from independent verification.
