## Project completion definition

A scene is only **master-complete** when:
- All planned source shots are approved;
- Correct approved versions are assembled in chronological order;
- Runtime matches the scene sheet;
- Dialogue/narration, SFX and music are mixed or intentionally omitted;
- Exported MP4 metadata is checked;
- The user reviews and approves the assembled scene.

Individual approved clips alone do not equal a fully finished scene master.
