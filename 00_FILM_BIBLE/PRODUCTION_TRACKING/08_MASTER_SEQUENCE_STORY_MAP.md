## Suggested story roles (not a dialogue script)

- S01: Introduce material hardship; Jones's neglect; the contrast between animal labour and poor treatment.
- S02: Old Major is the central speaker; he gives the ideological/emotional foundation for the rebellion.
- S03: Shift from frustration to collective action.
- S04: Establish the new rules and initial hope.
- S05: Set up the political conflict between Snowball and Napoleon.
- S06: Show the violent removal of Snowball and the end of open debate.
- S07: Contrast slogans/propaganda with growing exhaustion and scarcity.
- S08: Show external conflict and the cost of rebuilding.
- S09: Focus on fear, betrayal and Boxer's fate.
- S10: Deliver the final visual revelation that the new rulers resemble the old oppressors.
