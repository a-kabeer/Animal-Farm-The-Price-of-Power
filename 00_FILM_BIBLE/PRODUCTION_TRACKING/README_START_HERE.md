## Important distinction: master sequences vs one-minute working scenes

The uploaded blueprint defines **10 story sequences of 3 minutes each** (S01–S10). The current production conversation has created two **one-minute working scenes**:
- `ACT01_SCENE001` — Manor Farm Establishing (00:00–01:00)
- `ACT01_SCENE002` — The Animals' Work (01:00–02:00)

For tracking, these two one-minute scenes are provisionally treated as the opening two minutes of master sequence **S01 — The Hungry Farm (00:00–03:00)**. This mapping is a practical inference from their story content, not a separately signed-off screenplay decision. One minute of S01 remains to be planned. Do not confuse working `SCENE002` with master sequence `S02 — Old Major's Dream`.
