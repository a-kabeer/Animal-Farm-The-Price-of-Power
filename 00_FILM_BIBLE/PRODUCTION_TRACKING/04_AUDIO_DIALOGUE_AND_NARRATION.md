# 04 — AUDIO, DIALOGUE & NARRATION TRACKER

## A. What is actually defined now?

The uploaded blueprint specifies five post-production tracks:

- **A1:** Character dialogue / replacement dialogue
- **A2:** Narrator voice-over
- **A3:** Farm ambience and sound effects
- **A4:** Background music / score
- **A5:** Optional subtitles/captions

It also says Flow-generated audio should be reviewed, precise long dialogue should be recorded/generated separately and synchronized in CapCut or DaVinci Resolve, and music should sit below dialogue. The two completed one-minute shot lists (Scene 001 and Scene 002) specify **no dialogue or narration in every shot**. Some generated clips contain AAC audio, but AAC presence does not prove that every planned SFX/music cue is correctly present. A final audio mix is not confirmed.

## B. Narrator — not yet locked

The master blueprint includes a narrator VO track, but it does not define:
- The narrator's identity/name;
- Gender or age;
- Accent;
- Vocal quality or performance style;
- Exact narration lines;
- Which shots/sequences carry narration.

**Proposed direction for approval (not yet canon):** one mature British-English narrator, calm, measured, warm but unsentimental, with a restrained documentary/storytelling tone. The narrator should set context and bridge sequences, not explain every visible action. Keep the narrator separate from character voices.

No narrator lines are approved yet. Do not ask Flow to invent narration during video generation.

## G. Audio workflow to complete

1. Approve narrator voice direction and create a named reusable voice reference.
2. Create/select one voice per main character in Flow Character setup (where supported), following the voice bible above.
3. Write a dialogue/narration script with exact lines, speaker, shot ID, target duration and pronunciation notes.
4. Generate/record dialogue separately for long or precise lines.
5. Keep dialogue, narrator, SFX, music and captions on separate editor tracks.
6. Review every generated clip's audio; replace unwanted/garbled speech.
7. Mix music below dialogue; use silence intentionally.
8. Export and listen to the complete scene on headphones and phone speakers.

## H. Dialogue script approval gate

No line is canon until it has:
- Exact text;
- Approved speaker;
- Assigned scene/shot;
- Estimated spoken duration;
- Voice asset/reference;
- User approval.
